Alarms

Will the alarm ring if my iPhone is on silent or a Focus is on?

Morrow sets its wake-up alarm with Apple's alarm system (AlarmKit), the same kind of alarm the Clock app uses. Apple designed these alarms to sound when the Ring/Silent switch is set to silent and when a Focus such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb is on.

This needs the Alarms permission. If Morrow is not allowed to set alarms, it can't wake you.

Step cues and reminders during the morning are notifications, not alarms. They follow your notification settings and Focus. Spoken cues are off until you turn them on: in Morrow, open Settings, Cues and turn on Voice cues. They then follow that switch, not the silent switch.

I said no to alarms. How do I turn them back on?

Open the Settings app, tap Apps, then Morrow, and turn on Alarms. You can also get there from Morrow: Settings, Devices, Alarms opens the right page.

Once Alarms are on, open Morrow's Tomorrow screen to check the alarm time. While Morrow can't set alarms, Tomorrow says so.

My alarm didn't ring. What should I check?
  • Alarms are allowed for Morrow (Settings app, Apps, Morrow, Alarms).
  • Tomorrow showed an alarm time the night before. Morrow sets alarms only while Morrow Plus is active. If Tomorrow says "Your alarm isn't set", your subscription ends before that alarm: tap Manage subscription to turn auto-renew back on.
  • Your iPhone was on and had battery.

If all of that was fine, please email us with the time the alarm was set for and what happened.

During your morning

Where is the countdown on my Lock Screen?

When a morning starts, Morrow shows a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with the current step's countdown and whether you're on track. You can mark a step done or skip it from there.

If you don't see it, open the Settings app, tap Apps, then Morrow, and make sure Live Activities is on.

How do I plan a work-from-home morning?

Pick the Work from home routine when you set up Morrow, or later from Templates in the Routine tab. Morrow then plans back from when work starts instead of when you leave. Tomorrow shows "Work starts at", the last step is a desk check (laptop, charger, water, today's calendar), and the receipt shows when you got to your desk.

Any morning routine can end this way. Open it in the Routine tab and set Morning ends with to Starting work.

With Calendar leave time on, a work-from-home routine plans for work to start a little before your first event and adds no travel time, so it doesn't use your location.

How do learned times work?

Each step starts with the time you typed in. As you finish mornings, Morrow records how long each step really took. After 3 finished mornings, it uses the middle value of your last 9 timed runs of that step instead of your guess.

Steps you skipped or paused, and mornings you didn't finish, are left out. A single very long run counts as at most twice the planned time, so one odd morning doesn't throw off the plan.

Learning moves the alarm by at most 5 minutes a day, and Tomorrow tells you why it moved. Changing your leave time moves it right away.

To start a step over, open it in the Routine tab and tap Reset learned time. To start every step over, use Settings, Your data, Reset learned times. Your routines stay as they are.

How do I get the Apple Watch app?

The Apple Watch app comes with Morrow Plus. It needs watchOS 26 or later and a paired iPhone with Morrow on it. If your subscription has ended, the watch shows "Morrow Plus is off".

If it isn't on your watch yet, open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to Morrow. On the watch you'll see tomorrow's alarm and leave time, and during a morning you can mark steps done, skip them or pause from your wrist.

Morrow's Settings, Devices, Apple Watch shows whether the watch is connected.

Morrow Plus

Do I need a subscription?

Yes. Morrow needs Morrow Plus to plan and run your mornings. You set up your first routine and see your alarm first, then choose yearly, with a 14-day free trial, or monthly.

Everything in Morrow comes with it: the alarm that sets itself, Lock Screen pacing and cues, trimming when you fall behind, every routine including Work from home, calendar leave time, your full history and the weekly recap, widgets, Siri, the Apple Watch app and Mo's wardrobe.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with 14 days free if you haven't had the trial before. Nothing is charged during the trial.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged. Otherwise it becomes a yearly subscription. The monthly plan has no trial.

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. Then open Morrow and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Morrow Plus screen. If you're already in the app, Restore Purchases is also in Settings.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Morrow. From there you can cancel or switch between yearly and monthly. Morrow's Settings also has a Manage subscription button that opens the same page.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Morrow Plus keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. Tomorrow shows "Your alarm isn't set" for any alarm after that date.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Morrow stops setting alarms and reminders, the widgets and Apple Watch show "Morrow Plus is off", and the app opens on the Morrow Plus screen to ask you to subscribe again. A morning already in progress finishes first.

Your routines, history, learned times and Mo's wardrobe stay saved on your iPhone. Subscribe again, or tap Restore Purchases if your subscription is still active, and your mornings are planned as before.

What does Morrow Plus cost?

In the US, Morrow Plus is $39.99 a year with a 14-day free trial, or $9.99 a month. The App Store shows the price for your country before you confirm.

Your data

How do I delete all my data?

In Morrow, open Settings, then Your data, then Delete all data, and confirm. Without a subscription, tap Delete all data at the bottom of the Morrow Plus screen. Morrow deletes your routines, history, learned times and Mo's wardrobe, cancels its alarms and notifications, clears its widgets and Apple Watch app, and starts again from the welcome screen.

It isn't available while a morning is running. It doesn't cancel Morrow Plus, which belongs to your Apple Account.

Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes everything Morrow stored there. The privacy policy has the details.

Does Morrow sync between devices?

No. Morrow has no account and keeps your data on your iPhone. Your paired Apple Watch gets what it needs from the iPhone. Morrow's data is part of your iPhone backup, so restoring a new iPhone from that backup brings it along.
